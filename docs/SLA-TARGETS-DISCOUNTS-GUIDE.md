# SLA, Targets and Discounts: the idea in two minutes

Three settings. Each one answers one simple question.

| Setting | The question |
|---|---|
| **SLA** | Are we replying to leads fast enough? |
| **Targets** | Are we on track this month? |
| **Discount authority** | How much can each person take off a fee without asking? |

---

## SLA: a promise about speed

You decide how fast a lead must be answered. The CRM watches the clock for you.

**Example.** "Every new lead must get a first call within 1 hour."
Rahul enquires at 10:00. By 11:00 nobody has logged a call, so the lead is flagged **At risk** and the counsellor is told. If it is still untouched at 3:00 pm, the centre head is told as well.

That is all it is. You choose:

- **What to watch.** First reply to a new lead, a follow-up someone promised ("I'll call Thursday"), or a lead that has sat in the same status too long.
- **How many hours.** One hour, four hours, three days, whatever suits you.
- **Who gets told, and when.** For example the counsellor straight away, the centre head 4 hours later.
- **Working hours only.** Tick this so the clock stops at night, on Sundays and on holidays. A lead that arrives Saturday evening is not "late" by Sunday morning.

Good to know:

- **Set each centre's opening hours first** (on the same screen). If a centre has none, the clock never counts and nothing ever goes late.
- If several policies could apply to one lead, the one with the **highest priority number** wins. Give your catch-all a low number.
- Leads that have joined or been lost are never counted.
- When someone logs the call, the flag disappears on its own.

## Targets: the number you are aiming for

A target is just a number for the month, so you can see on the 15th whether you are ahead or behind.

**Example.** "Kochi: 40 admissions in November." You can also give each counsellor their own share, say 10 each.

You pick:

- **What to count:** leads, admissions, or revenue.
- **For whom:** the whole institute, one centre, or one person.

It shows on the dashboard as a progress bar. Each month starts empty, so you set the numbers fresh every month. A centre head can set their own centre's and their own team's numbers.

---

## Discount authority: how much each role can give

Each role gets a limit. Staying inside it needs nobody's permission. Going over it needs approval.

**Example.** Counsellor limit: 10% or ₹5,000, whichever is lower.

- ₹1,00,000 course, counsellor gives ₹4,000. Fine, it applies straight away.
- Same course, counsellor gives ₹8,000. That is under 10% but over ₹5,000, so it **waits for approval**. Until someone approves, the student is billed the full fee.

You can set a percentage, an amount, or "no limit" (for admins).

- A role with **no limit set can give nothing** without approval. Safe by default.
- The person approving needs a big enough limit themselves. A centre head with a ₹25,000 limit cannot approve a ₹50,000 discount.
- **Offers** (for example "Early Bird 5%") are discounts you have already approved, so picking one needs no sign-off.
- Nobody can raise their own limit.

---

## Who sets these up

In **Settings → Roles & Permissions**, tick one or more of these for a role such as Centre Head:

- **Manage SLA policies**
- **Set targets**
- **Set discount authority**

Each one gives access to that one screen and nothing else. Tick **Centre** for Targets so they only reach their own centre. For the other two, Centre or All works the same.

One thing to decide first: SLA policies and discount limits apply to **every centre**, so anyone you give them to can change them for everyone.
