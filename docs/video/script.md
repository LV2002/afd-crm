### 7. 00:49 — chapter (3.5s)

Chapter card **02 — Six words that mean something specific**

### 8. 00:52 — definitions (12.0s)

  **Lead** — A prospective student. The person, not the enquiry.
  **Enquiry** — One inbound event. Many enquiries, one lead.
  **Stage** — How far along they are in the funnel.
  **Temperature** — How likely they are to actually join.
  **Enrolment** — The commercial record: course, fee, instalments.
  **Student** — The academic record. Created when they pay.

### 9. 01:04 — contrast (13.0s)

**Stage and temperature are not the same thing**

  Stage: Where they are in the process  ·  is not  ·  Temperature: How likely they are to join

  _They move independently. Hot at Contacted, Cold at Demo Scheduled — both normal._

### 15. 01:39 — statement (8.5s)

### 16. 01:48 — chapter (3.5s)

Chapter card **05 — Finding and creating leads**

### 17. 01:51 — steps (8.0s)

HOW TO — **Find somebody**

  1. Open Leads
  2. Type part of a name or a phone number
  3. Press Enter — nothing happens until you do

### 18. 01:59 — masked (8.5s)

Diagram: +91 98••••3456 → click to reveal → recorded

  Phone numbers are masked in every list. Open the lead and click to reveal — and that reveal is recorded.

### 19. 02:08 — steps (17.5s)

HOW TO — **Create a walk-in**

  1. Leads → New lead
  2. Student name and Primary phone — the only required fields
  3. Centre, city, exams, courses, exam year
  4. Referred by — if somebody sent them, record it now

  _Referrals are among the best sources an institute has, and this is the only place they are captured._

### 20. 02:25 — chapter (3.5s)

Chapter card **06 — The one habit that matters**

### 21. 02:29 — statement (6.5s)

**If a call is not logged, it did not happen.**

### 22. 02:35 — steps (15.5s)

HOW TO — **Log an interaction**

  1. Type — Call, WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Walk-in, Meeting or Note
  2. Outcome — Connected, Not Reachable, Call Back Later, Interested…
  3. Notes — write for the colleague covering your leave
  4. Next action — what happens next
  5. Next follow-up — the date and time. Set this.

### 23. 02:51 — statement (7.5s)

A lead with no next follow-up drops off every queue in the system. Nobody chases it. Nobody notices.

### 24. 02:58 — chapter (3.5s)

Chapter card **07 — Three rules worth knowing early**

### 25. 03:02 — rule (12.0s)

RULE — **A duplicate is never rejected**

  Enter somebody who already exists and you get no error. The enquiry attaches to the person you already have, or the pair goes to Merge review.

### 26. 03:14 — rule (10.0s)

RULE — **Nothing is ever really deleted**

  Deleting hides a record and keeps who did it and why. It can be restored. Financial records cannot even be hidden.

### 27. 03:24 — rule (9.5s)

RULE — **Money is never edited**

  A payment recorded wrongly is corrected with a reversal that points at the original — so the history stays readable a year later.

Chapter card **08 — The pipeline**

### 29. 03:37 — stages (5.5s)

Diagram: the fourteen pipeline stages and their response targets

  Fourteen stages ship with the system. Drag a card on the Pipeline board to move somebody along.

### 30. 03:42 — statement (9.5s)

Dragging to Lost asks for a reason. “We lost them” teaches nobody anything; the reasons are the most useful column in the sources report.

### 31. 03:52 — chapter (3.5s)

Chapter card **09 — Confirming an admission**

### 32. 03:55 — steps (11.0s)

HOW TO — **Confirm admission**

  1. Course, Mode and Academic year — what they are actually joining
  2. Batch, if one is chosen
  3. Discount, if you are giving one
  4. Confirm admission → Yes, confirm it

### 33. 04:06 — warning (14.0s)

CAREFUL — **This is a one-way door**

  Confirming ends sales work on that lead and hands the family to accounts. Only an administrator can walk it back.

  _Do it when the admission is actually agreed — not when it looks likely._

### 34. 04:20 — statement (10.5s)

The course is asked here rather than copied from the lead, because what somebody enquired about and what they join are often not the same thing.

### 35. 04:31 — chapter (3.5s)

Chapter card **10 — Fees, payments and receipts**

### 36. 04:34 — steps (14.5s)

HOW TO — **Agree the plan**

  1. Course fee, discount, down payment
  2. Instalments — a date and an amount for each
  3. The panel tells you if they do not add up to the payable amount
  4. Print the agreement, have it signed, upload the signed copy

### 37. 04:49 — steps (11.0s)

HOW TO — **Record a payment**

  1. Admissions → the student → Record a payment
  2. Amount, Method, Received into, Reference
  3. Record payment → Yes, record it

  _Accounts and administrators only. Counsellors cannot record payments, deliberately._

### 38. 04:60 — statement (9.5s)

The first cleared payment creates the student record automatically. That is the second gate, and nobody has to remember to do it.

### 39. 05:09 — statement (7.5s)

Receipt numbers come from the database in an unbroken run — no gaps, no collisions, whoever is typing.

### 40. 05:17 — chapter (3.5s)

Chapter card **11 — Students and profile forms**

### 41. 05:20 — steps (14.0s)

HOW TO — **The profile form**

  1. On the lead: Create profile form link
  2. Copy link and send it to the student
  3. They fill it in — no login needed
  4. Their answers appear on the lead. Mark read when you have looked.
