# HR: employees, leave and leave slips

A new **HR** section in the sidebar. What each person sees depends on their role.

## Employee
**My HR** shows their own leave balances (sick and casual), the company holidays, their leave requests, and a form to apply.
Payslips will appear here once payroll is set up.

To apply: pick the kind (casual, sick, half day, loss of pay), the dates, and send. Weekly offs and company holidays inside the
range aren't counted. A request can be withdrawn while it's waiting, or after approval if the leave hasn't started.

## Manager
Whoever an employee **reports to** is told the moment they apply, and sees the request under **Waiting for your approval** on
their own My HR page. They approve it, or turn it down with a reason. The employee is told either way. If an employee has no
manager, HR approves.

Nobody can approve their own leave, even if they are HR.

## HR
**Employees** lists everybody with their leave left. **Leave** shows every request and lets HR decide any of them.
**Leave policy & holidays** holds the rules and the holiday list. HR sees no pay in this part.

An **approved leave slip** (a numbered page for each request) is visible to the employee, their manager and HR, and to nobody else.

## Setting people up
1. HR → Employees → **Add an employee**: name, number, designation, who they report to, joining date.
2. **CRM login**: pick their login so they can see their page. Give that login the **Employee** role (Settings → Users),
   which sees only HR. Existing roles already see My HR.
3. People whose leave balance is on an old payslip: enter the balance and the date it's as of. Counting starts after that date.

## The leave rules (all editable under Leave policy & holidays)
- 6 sick and 6 casual days a year. Balances reset on 1 January, with no carry over.
- In the year someone joins: the annual figure for the months left, to the nearest half day. The joining month counts unless you
  turn that off. No joining date means long-standing, so the full year.
- Leave beyond the balance isn't blocked. It shows as taken beyond the balance and is paid as loss of pay when payroll is run.
- Half days and loss of pay use no balance.
