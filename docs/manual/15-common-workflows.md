# Chapter 15 — Common workflows, end to end

These string together the individual procedures. Follow one all the way
through and you have done a day's work properly.

## 15.1 An enquiry arrives and becomes an admission

The whole journey, from first touch to the family paying.

### Who does what
Counsellor for steps 1–8, accounts for 9–10, academics after that.

### The steps

**1. The lead appears.**
From a Meta or Google ad, your website, Instagram, a walk-in or a phone
call. Ad and form leads arrive on their own, within seconds, and are
assigned by your rules. A walk-in you create yourself
(**Leads → New lead**).

**2. Check it is assigned to you.**
It is in your Dashboard queue under **New**. If it is sitting in
**Unassigned**, claim it.

**3. Ring them the same day.**
Stages have response targets — *Contacted* expects four hours. Missing
it puts the lead in **At risk**.

**4. Log the call.** Always.
Type, Outcome, Notes, Next action, **Next follow-up**. Without the
follow-up date the lead vanishes from everybody's queue.

**5. Move the stage.**
Drag the card on **Pipeline**, or set it on the lead. *Contacted* →
*Qualified* → *Demo Scheduled* and so on.

**6. Set the temperature honestly.**
Hot means *likely to join*, not *I had a nice chat*. Cold leads marked
Hot are how a pipeline becomes fiction.

**7. Keep following up.**
Every contact is a logged interaction with a new next-follow-up date.
Send the brochure, book the demo, answer the parents.

**8. Confirm the admission.** (Gate 1)
Only when it is genuinely agreed. **Confirm admission** → course, mode,
academic year, batch, any discount → **Yes, confirm it**.
**Sales work on this lead now stops.**

**9. Agree the fee plan.**
Down payment and instalments, totalling the payable amount. Print the
agreement, have it signed, upload the signed copy.

**10. Accounts records the first payment.** (Gate 2)
Amount, mode, which account, reference. **A student record is created
automatically.** The receipt is numbered and printable.

**11. Academics onboard the student.**
**Students → Onboarding** → collect documents, allocate a batch →
**Onboarding done**.

### How to tell it worked
The lead is at *Admission Confirmed*, the admission shows **Paid**, a
student exists with a code, and the onboarding queue is clear of them.

### Where it usually goes wrong
- A follow-up with no next date (step 4)
- Confirming an admission that was not actually agreed (step 8)
- Instalments that do not total the payable amount (step 9)
- Nobody pressing **Onboarding done** (step 11)

## 15.2 Your morning, in ten minutes

1. Open the **Dashboard**.
2. Work **Overdue** first. These people were promised a call that did not
   come.
3. Then **Due today**.
4. Then **New** — anything assigned to you and never contacted.
5. Then **At risk** — Hot with no next step, or an SLA breach.
6. Check **Chats** for replies.
7. Check the bell for notifications.

Every lead you touch gets a logged interaction and a next date. If a lead
genuinely needs nothing more, move it to *Lost* with a reason or *Parked*
— do not just leave it.

## 15.3 Somebody rings and you do not know who they are

1. Open **Leads**.
2. Paste the number into the search box and press **Enter**.
3. **If they exist**: open them, read the **Timeline** before saying
   anything, then take the call.
4. **If not**: **New lead**, name and number, then log the call. If they
   were referred, fill in **Referred by** now — nobody remembers later.

## 15.4 Taking over somebody's leads

When a counsellor leaves or goes on long leave.

1. Administrator: **Settings → Users** → **deactivate** them. Never
   delete — their name belongs on their history.
2. Find their open leads: **Leads**, filtered by assigned counsellor.
3. Reassign, or let them fall into **Unassigned** and work the queue.
4. Administrator: update **Settings → Assignment Rules** so new leads
   stop going to them.
5. For each lead you inherit, **read the Timeline before you ring**. It
   is the handover document.

## 15.5 A family asks for a discount

1. Agree it with them. You are not blocked.
2. Enter it on the fee plan with a **Discount name**, or pick a running
   offer.
3. **Within your limit** — it just applies.
4. **Above your limit** — it is recorded as pending. Somebody with
   authority approves or rejects it, with a note.
5. Once settled, print the agreement with the final figures.

Nobody has to say "let me ring my manager" in front of a family. The
authority is resolved afterwards.

## 15.6 Month end, for accounts

1. **Finance → Collections** — chase everything overdue.
2. **Finance → Record entry** — every expense for the month, with real
   dates and real descriptions.
3. **Finance → Account ledger** — reconcile each account against its
   statement.
4. **Finance → Monthly** — read the month: fees, other income, expenses
   by category.
5. **Insights → Targets** — against the month's numbers.
6. **Ad Performance** — cost per admission, if you advertise.

## 15.7 Launching a new batch

1. **Settings → Batches** — create it: name, centre, academic year,
   course, mode, dates, seats.
2. **Settings → Fee Structures** — make sure a fee exists for that
   course + centre + mode + academic year. **Do this before admissions
   start**, not during.
3. **Settings → Offers** — any early-bird discount, with its dates.
4. **Chats → Templates** — get the announcement template approved. Allow
   a day.
5. **Chats → Broadcasts** — build the audience, check the count, send.
6. Watch **Unassigned** as the replies come in.
