# Chapter 2 — Getting started

## 2.1 Signing in

### Goal
Get into the CRM for the first time.

### Before you start
An administrator must have created your account. You need the email
address they used and the password they gave you.

### Steps
1. Open the CRM address in your browser.
2. You land on the **Login** page.
3. Type your address in **Email**.
4. Type your password in **Password**.
5. Press **Sign in**.

### What you should see
The **Dashboard**, with the sidebar on the left. What is in that sidebar
depends on your role — see Chapter 3.

### Common mistakes and fixes
- **"Invalid login credentials"** — the email or password is wrong. There
  is no "forgot password" link on the login page; ask an administrator to
  reset it for you (Chapter 13).
- **You sign in and the sidebar is nearly empty.** Your role has not been
  given the permissions you expected. An administrator fixes this in
  Settings → Users.
- **You are signed out unexpectedly.** Sessions expire. Sign in again;
  nothing is lost.

## 2.2 The layout

Every screen has the same three parts.

**The sidebar, down the left.** This is the main navigation and it stays
put as you scroll. Each entry is a section of the system. Some entries
carry a **red number** — that is a work queue with items waiting in it.

**The main area.** Whatever you have opened.

**The top of the main area.** The page title, and the buttons that act on
the whole page (for example **New lead**, **Import**, **Export CSV** on
the leads list).

The sidebar only shows what you are allowed to use. If a colleague
describes a screen you cannot see, you do not have permission for it —
that is the system working, not a fault.

## 2.3 Your first day — a walkthrough

Follow this once and the rest of the manual will make sense.

### Goal
Get comfortable with the parts you will use every day.

### Before you start
Signed in, with at least the Counsellor role.

### Steps
1. **Read your Dashboard.** The top of it is your work queue: what is
   overdue, what is due today, what is new. This is the screen to start
   every morning on.
2. **Open Leads** in the sidebar. This is every lead you are allowed to
   see — for a counsellor, the ones assigned to you.
3. **Use the search box.** Type part of a name or a phone number and press
   Enter.
4. **Open one lead** by clicking it. Look at the left side: that is the
   lead's details, and you can edit them in place. Look at the right:
   that is where you log a call, add a task, and confirm an admission.
5. **Log an interaction.** In the **Log an interaction** panel pick a
   **Type** (Call, WhatsApp, Walk-in…), pick an **Outcome**, write a note,
   set **Next follow-up**, and save. This is the single most important
   habit in the system.
6. **Open Pipeline.** The same leads, arranged by stage. Useful for seeing
   where everybody is stuck.
7. **Open Chats.** Replies to the institute's WhatsApp messages arrive
   here.

### What you should see
After step 5, your note appears in the lead's **Timeline**, and the lead
shows a next follow-up date. If you set the follow-up for today or earlier,
it will be on your Dashboard queue.

### Common mistakes and fixes
- **You log a call but set no next follow-up.** The lead drops off your
  queue and nobody chases it. Always set a next action unless the lead is
  genuinely finished.
- **You edit the lead's details but forget to press Save.** Changes are not
  automatic.

## 2.4 Dates, times, money and phone numbers

- **Times** are shown in India Standard Time, always, whoever is looking.
- **Dates** appear as `4 Oct 2026` by default. An administrator can change
  the format.
- **Money** is in rupees, written `₹12,500`. Type amounts as plain numbers.
- **Phone numbers** are stored in international format
  (`+919847012345`). Type them however you like — with or without the
  `+91`, with or without spaces — and the system normalises them. Numbers
  that cannot be understood are rejected.
- **A masked number** looks like `+91 98••••3456`. See Chapter 6 for how
  to reveal one.
