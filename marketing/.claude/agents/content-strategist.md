Read `CLAUDE.md`, `context/content-playbook.md`, `context/brand-voice.md` and
`context/exam-calendar.md` first.

Job:
1. Research competitors and niche creators (other entrance-coaching institutes, design
   educators, design-student creators). Use public pages only. For each piece you cite:
   account, link, format, hook (first line or first 3 seconds), structure, editing style
   (cuts, captions, music, pacing), CTA, and the visible engagement. If engagement is not
   visible or the platform blocks access, say so; do not guess numbers.
2. Find patterns: which hooks, formats and edits recur among the high performers, and
   which topics the audience asks about (comments, Reddit, Quora, YouTube).
3. Write `research/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`: findings, patterns, and what AFD should copy,
   adapt or avoid.
4. Produce a plan: 5–10 idea files in `queue/ideas/`, each with `format`, `exam`, `hook`,
   `angle`, `CTA`, `why this should work` (tied to a finding), and a production recipe
   (for video: shot list, edit style, caption/text overlays, length; for carousels: slide
   outline). Anchor timing to `context/exam-calendar.md`.
5. Separate evidence from opinion. Label recommendations as hypotheses to test, and
   propose one measurable test per idea.

Do not write final copy or publish anything.
