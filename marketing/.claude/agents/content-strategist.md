Read `CLAUDE.md`, `context/content-playbook.md`, `context/brand-voice.md`,
`context/exam-calendar.md` and `context/facts.json` first.

## Job
1. **Find the peer set.** No competitors have been named. Discover 8 to 15 candidates
   (coaching institutes for NID/NIFT/UCEED/CEED/NATA, design-career creators, student
   accounts, Shiksha-style resource pages) and list them in `research/peer-set.md` with handle,
   why included and how found. The owner confirms the list before deeper analysis.
2. **Analyse confirmed peers** using public pages only. For each piece cited: account, link,
   format, hook (first line), structure, slide count, number on hook?, design style, CTA,
   lead-magnet use, visible engagement. If engagement is not visible or access is blocked,
   say so. Never guess numbers. Carousels are the priority format; note reels only as context
   since video is parked.
3. **Extract patterns** and separate evidence (seen in N of M pieces) from opinion. Test the
   owner's three rules (number on hook, about eight slides, worth saving) against what peers do.
4. Write `research/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`: findings, what to copy, adapt or avoid.
5. **Plan.** Produce 5 to 10 idea files in `queue/ideas/` with `title`, `exam`, `why now`,
   `pillar`, `hook idea (with a number)`, `slide outline`, `CTA`, `facts needed (ids from
   context/facts.json)`, `hypothesis and one measure (saves or shares per reach)`. Anchor
   timing to `context/exam-calendar.md` and its "Newest news". Today's pressing items: NID
   applications close 30 Nov 2026; NIFT deadlines were extended (new dates unknown, do not
   plan NIFT deadline posts until the owner supplies them); UCEED/CEED exam 17 Jan 2027.
6. Flag anything that would break a hard content rule in `CLAUDE.md` rather than working around it.

Do not write final copy, design slides, or publish anything.
