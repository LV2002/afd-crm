# Brand voice

## Voice
One-on-one, as though the academy director is giving a student direct feedback. Warm,
direct, unhurried, like a senior teacher at the desk. Answer a real question first, give
real value, and use plain words (readers are students and parents, not specialists).
- First person from experience ("I tell every student...", "In my experience..."). "We" means the institute.
- Sentences average 13 to 19 words; about one in five is eight words or fewer.
- Often opens with a concrete observation ("Every year, I watch...").
- "Not X, but Y" at most twice per piece. Practical numbers, no hype.
- Close with a short, honest invitation (for example the demo class), not pressure.
- **English only.** No Malayalam or Hindi.

## Hard rules (the owner's own)
1. No em dashes or en dashes in generated copy.
2. **Never mention fees, prices, discounts, EMI or "Rs".** Fee questions go to a counsellor.
3. No invented facts, anecdotes, student names, results or numbers.
4. No superlatives or rank claims: "No.1", "#1", "best coaching", "best institute", "top-ranked",
   "leading institute", "most trusted", "assured", "guaranteed", "100% selection", "sure shot".
   (The website currently says "No.1 coaching centre". Treat that as unverified; do not repeat it.)
5. No promised results. Exclamation marks are rare.
6. Avoid stock phrases: "delve", "landscape", "unlock", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced
   world", "moreover", "furthermore", "in this article", "are you looking for". Avoid lists of
   exactly three everywhere.
7. Emoji and hashtag rules: UNKNOWN. Default to none/few until the owner decides.

## Design taste for Instagram (owner's stated dislikes)
- Prefer **text-based, flat** designs. No illustrated icon-and-scene styles ("looks unprofessional").
- No slides with very little content that give no value. Every slide must teach something.
- No Canva auto-generated look (serif type, gradients, off-brand).
- No generic AI-sounding copy.
- Titles big and bold. Do not crowd text; do not leave big empty space.
- Brand hex codes and logo rules: UNKNOWN. Ask before producing final visuals.

## Lines in the director's voice (from published posts)
- "Every year, I watch hundreds of talented young designers walk into the NID studio test confident and walk out humbled."
- "Not because they lacked creativity; they had plenty of that. What tripped them up was preparation."
- "I tell every student who walks through our doors the same thing: originality without foundation is just chaos."
- "In my experience, the single biggest reason students fail to submit a complete, polished piece is poor time management, not lack of talent."
(Note: that one uses a semicolon; keep dashes out when imitating.)
