# Carousel design system

Status: the Marketing Brain generator makes **text only**; slide rendering to JPEG is not built.
Image size and exact brand values are UNKNOWN. Ask before final visuals.

## Look (old carousels, owner-approved direction, Sep to Oct 2026)
- Flat design only. Big display type (Gagalin), highlighter treatment, one accent colour per exam.
- Inside slides: text, not pictures. Allowed blocks: numbered colour chips, arrow bullets,
  tick/cross rows, key-pill steps, comparison columns, tables, a stat, question cards, strip
  callouts, pills, dark notes, a closing end line. Flat geometric diagrams only when a question needs a picture.
- Not allowed: icons, scenes, charts, calendars, polls, recaps. Illustrated style was tried and
  rejected ("looks unprofessional"). Canva auto designs rejected (serif type, gradients).
- Layout: margins 72 to 88 px; no text under 30 px; fixed sizes per element type, never
  auto-scaled; spread content down the slide; no line above slide titles; per-slide counter.
- Covers: three skins (light, dark, block), series badge, mascot, "N slides >" cue.
- Mascot: pencil, four character sheets, 10 expressions. Mandatory on covers, sparing on
  content slides, never on CTA slides, never cropped, never touching the edge or overlapping text.
  Always use the cut-outs, never the cropped older files.
- CTA slide: dark, big headline with yellow highlight, sub line, exam chips, "Call or WhatsApp",
  phone pill, www.afdindia.com, source line bottom-left, "Save for later" pill.
  Phones: Kochi 98954 84652, Kannur 98954 84651 (these are the confirmed 9895484652 and 9895484651; an earlier export wrongly called 98954 84652 outdated, but it is the same number).

## Sizes (assumption, confirm)
Feed posters were 1080x1350 (4:5). Instagram crops a carousel to the first image's shape.
Assume 1080x1350 JPEG until the owner says otherwise. Publish bundle expects 2 to 10 public JPEGs
with alt text up to 1000 chars.
