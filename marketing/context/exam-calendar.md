## Yearly pattern (from the 2027 documents)
- NIFT: register mid Oct to mid Nov, exam second Sunday of Jan, result Feb/Mar, Situation Test Mar/Apr, final result early May.
- NID: apply Sep to end Nov, Prelims third week of Dec, result Mar, Mains later.
- UCEED/CEED: register 1 to 31 Oct, same Sunday exam mid Jan, results early Mar.
- NATA: weekends Apr to Jul (site text). JEE Paper 2: two attempts a year (site text).
- Exam dates, syllabus and patterns change every year, so old content must be refreshed each cycle.
